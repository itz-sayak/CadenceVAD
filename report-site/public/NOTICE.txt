CadenceVAD
Copyright 2026 Sayak Dutta

CadenceVAD is authored by Sayak Dutta. New work in this repository includes the
causal learnable modulation filterbank, the AVA-Speech single-protocol
evaluation harness, the conversation-mixture data pipeline, the pairwise ranking
objective, the causal running-normalisation input stage, and the portable
low-latency C runtime for Windows, Linux and macOS.

DERIVED WORK
------------
CadenceVAD is derived from FlashVAD, Copyright 2026 Himanshu Maurya, used under
the MIT License: https://github.com/oss-codes/flashvad

The retained `cadencevad-v0.1` reference checkpoint is the FlashVAD v0.1
checkpoint. It was trained by Himanshu Maurya, is redistributed here unmodified
under CC BY 4.0, and is used only as a comparison baseline. It is not a
CadenceVAD-trained model. See models/cadencevad-v0.1/MODEL_LICENSE.md.

THIRD-PARTY DATA AND MODELS
---------------------------
The FlashVAD v0.1 reference checkpoint was trained using derived data from:

- FLEURS, Google LLC, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
- AMI Meeting Corpus, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/corpus/
- MUSAN, David Snyder, Guoguo Chen, and Daniel Povey, licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  https://www.openslr.org/17/

Weak frame targets were generated with Silero VAD, distributed under the MIT
License: https://github.com/snakers4/silero-vad

CadenceVAD models are trained on:

- LibriSpeech, Vassil Panayotov et al., licensed under CC BY 4.0.
  https://www.openslr.org/12/
- MUSAN, licensed under CC BY 4.0. https://www.openslr.org/17/
- Room impulse responses and isotropic noises (OpenSLR 28), CC BY 4.0.
  https://www.openslr.org/28/

Frame targets are produced by an ensemble of Silero VAD (MIT) and FireRedVAD
(Apache-2.0). Neither model's code nor weights are included here.
